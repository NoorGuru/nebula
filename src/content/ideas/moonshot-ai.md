## The problem

Frontier AI looked like a closed club: the best reasoning and coding models sat behind expensive American APIs that developers could rent but never inspect, fine-tune, or self-host — with context windows too short for real codebases and pricing set in Silicon Valley.

## How it works

Founded in March 2023 in Beijing by Yang Zhilin with Zhou Xinyu and Wu Yuxin, Moonshot first broke out with the Kimi chatbot's long-context handling, then went all-in on open weights. Kimi K2 (July 11, 2025) — a 1-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with ~32B active parameters under a modified-MIT license — became the fastest-downloaded model on Hugging Face a day after launch, an "open-weight DeepSeek moment" for agentic coding. Kimi K3, released July 16, 2026, scales that bet to a 2.8-trillion-parameter natively multimodal flagship with a ~1M-token context window, reported as China's largest open-weight model and expected to perform at par with Anthropic's Opus 4.8.

## Pain points

Prohibitive inference costs, lock-in to closed foreign APIs, short context windows, and zero inspectability of the models developers build on — exactly what downloadable, fine-tunable Kimi weights address.

## Business model

Aggressively priced API and platform access to Kimi models plus enterprise adoption: free open-weight downloads act as distribution, paid inference and high-volume usage monetise second.

## Challenges

US labs keep raising the spend bar while chip export controls squeeze Chinese compute access; open weights are hard to monetise against closed competitors; and a ~$20B valuation on ~$200M annualized revenue prices in growth that must still materialise — with a Hong Kong IPO adding public-market scrutiny.

## Funding

- Raised: ~$4B total across rounds — a 2024 mega-round of ~$1B+ led by Alibaba, follow-ons from Tencent, Gaorong, and IDG, and a ~$2B 2026 round.
- Valuation: ~$20B (2026 round snapshot; figures move fast and diverge across sources).

## Latest — July 2026

Moonshot released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight flagship that independent reviewers placed at or near frontier level on agentic-coding and long-horizon build tasks — the strongest evidence yet that the open-vs-closed gap has nearly closed — with a full open-weight drop following days later.
